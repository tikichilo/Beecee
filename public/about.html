Who We Are

Zambian-Owned. Built On 28+ Years in Transport.

Bee Cee Logistics Ltd is a Zambian-registered company providing car hire, customs clearing, removals, relocation, general supply, and project logistics consultancy. We're owned by Zambian professionals who've spent more than 28 years in the transport industry, working to a standard that meets international expectations.

We develop comprehensive travel and tour, logistics consultancy, and relocation services tailored to each customer — starting by learning their exact internal and external requirements, then structuring the job around them. That's how we've stayed one of the most consistent car hire and logistics providers operating in Zambia.

Market Position

One of Zambia's Leading Independent Providers.

Since establishment, Bee Cee Logistics Ltd has been recognized as one of the most successful independent providers of car hire and tour, removals, relocation, construction, freight forwarding, customs clearing, and general supply in Zambia. We offer both self-drive and chauffeur-driven car hire, plus a fleet of buses for day or night staff shift movements, school pickups, and school tours.
